# Team Ideation

## Brainstormed Ideas

1. **AI Habit Tracker** – An application that helps users build good habits, track daily progress, and receive personalized suggestions.
2. **Document Summarizer** – An AI-powered tool that summarizes long documents into short, easy-to-understand key points.
3. **AI Decision Helper** – An application that helps users make better decisions by analyzing options, comparing pros and cons, and providing structured recommendations. ✅ **Chosen**

## Chosen Problem Statement

People often struggle to make decisions because they have multiple options, limited time, and difficulty evaluating the advantages and disadvantages of each choice.

**AI Decision Helper** aims to solve this problem by using AI to analyze a user's decision, identify potential benefits and drawbacks, compare available options, and provide a clear, structured recommendation.

## MVP Features

1. **Decision Input:** Users can describe a problem or decision and enter the available options.
2. **AI-Powered Analysis:** AI generates the pros and cons of each option.
3. **Option Comparison:** The application presents the available choices in a structured, easy-to-understand format.
4. **Smart Recommendation:** AI suggests a suitable option based on the user's stated priorities.
5. **Results Display:** Users can view the analysis and recommendation on a simple, responsive interface.
