# MVP Concept - EcoRide Sustainable Ride-Sharing Platform

## Problem Statement

Many people commute daily using private cars, which contributes to traffic congestion, air pollution, and higher transportation costs. Finding affordable and environmentally friendly shared rides can be difficult.

EcoRide is a sustainable ride-sharing platform that connects drivers with passengers traveling in the same direction. The MVP will help users find shared rides, reduce travel expenses, and support environmentally responsible transportation.

## Target Users

- **Daily commuters:** People who travel regularly to work or school.
- **Drivers:** People who want to share travel costs with passengers.
- **Students:** People looking for affordable transportation.
- **Environmentally conscious users:** People who want to reduce their carbon footprint.

## Core Features

1. **Ride Listing:** Drivers can create ride listings with departure locations, destinations, dates, times, available seats, and prices.
2. **Ride Search:** Passengers can search for available rides by entering their departure location, destination, and travel date.
3. **Ride Booking:** Passengers can book available seats on a ride.
4. **Ride Management:** Drivers can view their rides, while passengers can view and cancel their bookings.
5. **Environmental Impact Summary:** Display estimated CO₂ emissions saved through shared rides using a simplified calculation.

## Constraints

- The MVP will focus on a limited geographical area.
- User authentication will be simplified or omitted in the initial prototype.
- Online payments will not be implemented; ride costs will be agreed upon between drivers and passengers.
- Ride listings and bookings will use a simple database or temporary storage.
- Location matching and environmental impact calculations will use basic logic rather than advanced algorithms.
- The initial version will prioritize core functionality and usability over advanced features such as live GPS tracking, in-app messaging, and automated route optimization.
